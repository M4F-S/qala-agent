section: Article 1
section_title: GCC Unified Customs Tariff Standard Duty Rate
page: 12
numerical: 0.05
text: A standard customs tariff rate of 5.0% (0.0500) applies to all commercial goods imported into UAE mainland on declared Cost, Insurance, and Freight (CIF) valuation, unless explicitly exempt under GCC unified agreements.
